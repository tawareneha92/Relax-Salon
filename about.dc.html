More than a salon. A place to relax, learn & transform.

Relax Salon & Academy sits on Sinhgad Road in Nanded City, Pune, and works on two sides of the same idea: beauty services that fit the person in the chair, and beauty education that gives people real, usable skills.

The salon covers hair, skin, beauty and grooming, including bridal and occasion work. The academy trains beginners and working professionals in hair styling and beauty practice. Both run on the same standard of hygiene, honesty about what will suit you, and time taken to do the job properly.

The approach

Professional beauty care, tailored to you.

Every service starts with a short conversation: what you like, what you have tried before, how much time you spend on your hair or skin at home. The recommendation follows from that, not from whatever is on offer that week.

The academy grew out of the same habit of explaining. Students learn on real technique and real client expectations, so that what they practise in training is what the salon floor actually asks for.
